# 08 — Story Strategy: OGERY Camping Fan

## Chosen framework: PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solve) + AIDA close

**Why PAS wins here:** the product solves three sharp, instantly-relatable pains
(heat, darkness, dead phone). PAS lets us open on a pain the viewer has *felt* in
the first 3 seconds — the strongest possible TikTok hook — then stack the pains
(agitate) so the 3-in-1 payoff lands as pure relief. UGC + PAS is the highest-
converting combo for a demo-able TikTok Shop gadget. We close AIDA-style with a
direct "tap the basket" CTA.

## Beat map (target 30s, vertical 9:16, Thai)
| Time | Beat | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | **Hook / Problem** | Boss in a hot, dark tent, sweating, phone nearly dead |
| 3–7s | **Agitate** | Fast cuts stacking all three pains |
| 7–11s | **Solve — intro** | Boss reveals OGERY: "ตัวเดียวจบ" |
| 11–15s | **Demo 1** | Fan airflow + LED ring lights the tent (relief) |
| 15–19s | **Demo 2** | Phone charges on wireless pad + power-bank base |
| 19–23s | **Demo 3** | Hang on tent pole + remote control |
| 23–27s | **Proof / recap** | 3-in-1 payoff, comfortable glowing campsite |
| 27–30s | **CTA (Action)** | Product beauty shot + "กดตะกร้าเหลืองเลย" |

## Specs
- Platform: TikTok (Shop) · Aspect: 9:16 · Duration: 30s · Language: Thai
- Tone: authentic, energetic-but-warm, friend-recommending-gear
- Claims policy: only visually-verifiable features; no invented numbers.
  If the user supplies runtime/capacity later, insert at the Demo-2 beat.

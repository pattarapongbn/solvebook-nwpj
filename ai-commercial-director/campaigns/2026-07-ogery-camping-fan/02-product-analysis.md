# 02 — Product Analysis: OGERY Camping Fan

## Product Summary
OGERY is a rugged, rechargeable 3-in-1 outdoor device: a tilt-adjustable fan, a
bright circular LED lantern, and a high-capacity battery base that charges phones
(wireless pad + cable) and comes with a remote. One gadget replaces a camping fan,
a lantern, and a power bank — built for tents, but just as useful during a home
blackout or a sweaty Thai night.

## Customer Avatar
- **"เบส" (Bass), 29, Bangkok office worker & weekend camper.** Drives to national
  parks 1–2× a month with his girlfriend/friends. Hates two things about camping:
  a stuffy hot tent at night and a phone that dies with no outlet in sight.
- Buying behavior: discovers gear on TikTok, reads/watches reviews, buys on TikTok
  Shop / Shopee when a demo convinces him it "just works."

## Pain Points (ranked)
1. **Tent gets unbearably hot & stuffy at night** → OGERY fan moves air all night
   on battery. (proof: fan runs untethered, hangs above you in the tent)
2. **Phone dies outdoors, no power outlet** → base is a power bank with wireless +
   cable charging. (proof: phone shown charging in-scene)
3. **Camp is pitch black, juggling a separate lantern** → built-in LED ring lights
   the whole site. (proof: bright ring light on device)
4. **Too much gear to carry** → one unit = fan + light + power bank. (proof: the
   3-in-1 form factor itself)

## Desired Outcome
A cool, well-lit, worry-free campsite where the phone never dies — comfort and
control outdoors, packed into a single grab-and-go gadget.

## Emotional Trigger
**Relief / comfort-in-control** — the exhale of "everything I need is handled" when
you're far from home. Secondary: the small pride of having the *smart* gear.

## Objections & Proof
| Objection | Neutralizing proof (must be real — confirm specs) |
|---|---|
| "Battery won't last the night" | State real runtime hours + capacity (TO CONFIRM) |
| "Too weak to actually cool me" | Show hair/shirt moving in the airflow on camera |
| "One more thing that breaks outdoors" | Show rugged casing, rain/drop handling (confirm IP rating before claiming) |
| "Wireless charging is a gimmick" | Show phone battery % rising on the pad in real time |
| "Overpriced for a fan" | Reframe as 3 products in 1 (fan + lantern + power bank) |

## Competitive Position
Vs. a plain USB clip fan: OGERY adds light + real power-bank capacity + remote.
Vs. a power station: OGERY is lighter, cheaper, purpose-built for airflow + light.
The wedge: **the only one that cools you, lights the camp, AND keeps the phone
alive — from one charge.**

## Marketing Angle
> "แค่ตัวเดียว จบทุกปัญหาในเต็นท์" — One gadget kills the three things that ruin a
> night outdoors: heat, darkness, and a dead phone.

# 12 — QA Checklist

## Product continuity
- [ ] Product in every scene matches the locked description block and P001 references
- [ ] No invented claims — every claim traces to 02-product-analysis.md
- [ ] Label/logo text specified correctly (and in negatives to prevent garbling)

## Character continuity
- [ ] Every scene uses the approved Character Bible, zero attribute drift
- [ ] Consistency block pasted verbatim in every scene prompt featuring the character
- [ ] Wardrobe per scene matches the storyboard continuity map

## Story
- [ ] Hook lands within first 3 seconds
- [ ] Emotion escalates; product introduced naturally; proof shown, not told
- [ ] Clear CTA in final scene
- [ ] Total runtime matches 08-story-strategy.md target

## Technical
- [ ] Every scene prompt is self-contained (no references the video model can't see)
- [ ] Aspect ratio, duration, camera and lighting language consistent throughout
- [ ] Voice / music / subtitle prompts present and separate from scene prompts
- [ ] All deliverable files exist and 00-state.md is up to date
